The business model is structurally rewarded for your health improvement at every level — making WHOOP a genuine health partner, not a passive financial safety net.

A structurally different model

Traditional insurers profit when you don't claim. WHOOP profits when your BHS rises. Every incentive points in the same direction.

📈

When your BHS improves

Your premium falls and WHOOP's claims costs fall with it — creating direct economic value for both parties simultaneously.

You purchase gym memberships, nutritionist sessions or recovery tools. WHOOP earns a commission. Your BHS improves further next month.

Healthier customers attract more health-conscious members, lower the claims pool, and make the model more profitable — enabling lower base premiums over time.
